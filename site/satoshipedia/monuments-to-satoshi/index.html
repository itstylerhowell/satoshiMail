Monuments to Satoshi

Budapest and Lugano

Budapest
Bust, 2021
Lugano
Statue by Valentina Picozzi

Satoshi has been commemorated with statues.[1]

  • Budapest: a bust of Satoshi was installed in Budapest, Hungary, in 2021.[1]
  • Lugano: a statue by Valentina Picozzi in Lugano, Switzerland. It was stolen and thrown into the lake, and later recovered.[1]
