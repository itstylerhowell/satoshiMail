The Satoshi Nakamoto Institute (SNI) was founded in November 2013 “to advance and preserve knowledge of Bitcoin's history, economics, and technology”.[1] Its Complete Satoshi collection of emails and forum posts, with the surrounding threads, is the core of Satoshi Mail.[2]

SNI also publishes The Quotable Satoshi, a library of papers and the Mempool essays.[1] It notes that the 2014 “I am not Dorian Nakamoto” post's authenticity “demands scrutiny”.[2] See The Quotable Satoshi and nakamoto-archive.
