Estimates of the bitcoins in Satoshi's possession range from 750,000 to 1.1 million; the wallet has been untouched since 2010.[1] Another estimate puts what he mined at about one million.[2]

Moving the earliest bitcoins mined, or signing a message with the key associated with them, has been proposed as proof of being Satoshi (Identity of Satoshi Nakamoto).[1] His 10-bitcoin payment to Hal Finney was the first bitcoin transaction.[3]
