Precursors of Bitcoin

Ecash, Hashcash, b-money, bit gold and RPOW

Period
1983 – 2004

Bitcoin combined ideas that cryptographers had been developing for two decades. Satoshi said as much to Adam Back when he learned of b-money: “my ideas start from exactly that point.” (the email).

  • Ecash (1983): David Chaum's anonymous cryptographic electronic money, using blind signatures; the money was cryptographically signed by a bank.[1]
  • Hashcash (1997): Adam Back's proof-of-work scheme for spam control.[2] The white paper cites it.[3]
  • b-money (1998): Wei Dai's “anonymous, distributed electronic cash system”.[4] It is reference [1] in the white paper.[3]
  • Bit gold (1998): Nick Szabo's mechanism for a decentralised digital currency.[5]
  • RPOW (2004): Hal Finney's reusable proof-of-work system, the first of its kind.[6]

The white paper presents its own answer as a solution to “the double-spending problem using a peer-to-peer network”.[3]
