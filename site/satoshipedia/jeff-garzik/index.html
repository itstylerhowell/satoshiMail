Jeff Garzik is a software developer who, as jgarzik, is one of the most frequent posters in the forum threads Satoshi took part in (his messages). Through 2010 he proposed patches and features there, such as “[PATCH] increase block size limit” (the thread).

Garzik was among those who disputed Craig Wright's claim to be Satoshi.[1]
