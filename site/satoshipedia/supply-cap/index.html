21 million supply cap

From Satoshipedia, the Satoshi Mail encyclopedia

21 million supply cap

Bitcoin's fixed maximum supply

Limit
21,000,000 BTC
Halving
Every 210,000 blocks
First stated
January 2009

Bitcoin's supply is capped at 21 million coins, and the block reward halves every 210,000 blocks.[1] Satoshi set out the schedule in the v0.1 release notes, “with the amount cut in half every 4 years” (release notes), and repeated it in the 0.3 announcement: “Bitcoin's total circulation is limited to 21 million coins.” (the announcement).

When users worried that so few coins could not serve the world, he pointed to divisibility: “Eventually at most only 21 million coins for 6.8 billion people in the world if it really gets huge. But don't worry, there are another 6 decimal places that aren't shown” (the post). See Satoshi (unit).

On lost coins he wrote: “Lost coins only make everyone else's coins worth slightly more. Think of it as a donation to everyone.” (the post).

When Mike Hearn asked in 2009 where “24 million coins” came from, Satoshi replied that “there's only going to be 21 million coins for the whole world” (the emails).
