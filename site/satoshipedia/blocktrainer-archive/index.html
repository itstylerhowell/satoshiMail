Blocktrainer archive is the name used here for “Satoshi Nakamoto’s Emails & Posts: The Full Archive”, a searchable collection published by Blocktrainer. It brings together Satoshi's emails, forum posts and mailing-list contributions in date order, links each one to its original source, includes posts by early correspondents and forum users where they give context, and offers a German translation of every entry.[1]

Compared with this archive

On 20 October 2026 its 4,236 entries were checked against Satoshi Mail's data, and every one of them is also here. Its forum and mailing-list entries match the Satoshi Nakamoto Institute's collection post for post: 3,831 BitcoinTalk posts in 259 threads, 14 P2P Foundation posts and 72 mailing-list emails. Its private emails match nakamoto-archive's, except that it has no emails with Adam Back and includes only Satoshi's side of his exchanges with Mike Hearn and Dustin Trammell.

It dates Satoshi's email to Laszlo Hanyecz 11 December 2009, where nakamoto-archive's source gives May 2010 (the email). Conversations in Satoshi Mail that it includes link to it.
