Peter Todd

Bitcoin developer named by HBO

Known for
Early Bitcoin development
Named by
Money Electric (HBO, 2024)

Peter Todd is a Canadian software developer and an early contributor to Bitcoin. Cullen Hoback's 2024 HBO documentary Money Electric: The Bitcoin Mystery named him as Satoshi. Todd denied it, calling the claim “ludicrous”.[1] He does not appear in Satoshi's correspondence. See Media investigations.
