Bitcoin Pizza Day

From Satoshipedia, the Satoshi Mail encyclopedia

Bitcoin Pizza Day

22 May 2010

Date
22 May 2010
Buyer
Laszlo Hanyecz
Price
10,000 BTC

Bitcoin Pizza Day marks 22 May 2010, when Laszlo Hanyecz paid 10,000 bitcoins for two pizzas, worth about $41 at the time.[1] It was the first known commercial transaction using bitcoin, later celebrated as Bitcoin Pizza Day.[2]

A few days earlier Satoshi had written to Hanyecz asking him to slow down his GPU mining, which was earning him far more coins than CPU miners could (the email).
