The identity of Satoshi Nakamoto has never been established. Satoshi gave few personal details, used anonymous email services (Satoshi's accounts and keys), and stopped writing in 2011. The name might belong to a team of people.[1]

What Satoshi said

On his P2P Foundation profile he claimed to be a 37-year-old man living in Japan. His writing uses British spellings, and his forum posts drop to almost none between 5 and 11 a.m. GMT (writing style).[1] The one later message attributed to his account reads, in full: “I am not Dorian Nakamoto.” (the post).

Proof

Proposed proofs include signing a message with the key associated with the first bitcoins, or moving the earliest bitcoins mined (Satoshi's coins).[1]
