Ray Dillinger

From Satoshipedia, the Satoshi Mail encyclopedia

Ray Dillinger

Reviewer of the November 2008 code

Known for
Reviewing the pre-release code
Handles
Bear, Cryddit

Ray Dillinger took part in the Cryptography mailing list discussion of the white paper and was one of three people Satoshi sent his source files to in November 2008, along with Hal Finney and James A. Donald. He was copied as bear@sonic.net (Finney's reply).

Dillinger, posting as Cryddit, later published the main files from that November 2008 code on BitcoinTalk; nakamoto-archive keeps a copy[1] (Pre-release source code).
