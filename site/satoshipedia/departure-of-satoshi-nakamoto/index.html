December 2010 – April 2011

Satoshi withdrew from Bitcoin gradually between December 2010 and April 2011, handing the project to Gavin Andresen and other developers.

Contents
  1. Last public activity
  2. Last emails
  3. Timeline

Last public activity

His last forum post, on 12 December 2010, announced version 0.3.19 and its denial-of-service limits (the post). It came a day after his warning that “WikiLeaks has kicked the hornet's nest” (WikiLeaks and Bitcoin). His last commit followed on 15 December (r202).

Last emails

He kept writing privately into 2011. To Mike Hearn, on 23 April 2011: “I've moved on to other things. It's in good hands with Gavin and everyone.” (the email). To Andresen, on 26 April 2011, with the alert key: “I've moved on to other things and will probably be unavailable.” (the email). This archive holds no later email from him.
