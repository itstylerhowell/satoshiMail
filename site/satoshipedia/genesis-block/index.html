The genesis block is block 0 of the Bitcoin blockchain, hard-coded into the software and dated 3 January 2009. Embedded in it is the text “The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks”.[1]

The headline serves as a timestamp, and has been read as a derisive comment on the instability caused by fractional-reserve banking.[2] Satoshi described it as “a hardcoded block that you start out with” (the post).

The first open-source client, v0.1, was released on 9 January 2009.[3] Satoshi saw newspapers as natural timestamps: in January 2009 he told Dustin Trammell that publishing hashes in “a magazine or newspaper” would “work a lot easier in court” (the email).
