Satoshi used several accounts, mostly through services that did not require a real identity.

Email

  • satoshi@anonymousspeech.com: his earliest address, used to write to Adam Back and Wei Dai in August 2008.
  • satoshi@vistomail.com: used to announce the white paper on the Cryptography list, bitcoin-list, and his emails with Hal Finney and Dustin Trammell.
  • satoshin@gmx.com: used from 2009 with Martti Malmi, Mike Hearn and Gavin Andresen. It is the address printed on the white paper at bitcoin.org.[1]

Accounts and keys

He posted as satoshi on BitcoinTalk, committed as s_nakamoto on SourceForge, and kept a profile on the P2P Foundation site. He published a PGP public key in 2008.[2] He also held the network alert key, which he passed to Gavin Andresen in April 2011 (the email).

In September 2014 his P2P Foundation account was compromised through an email hack.[3]
