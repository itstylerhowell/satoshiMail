bitcoin.org is the domain Satoshi registered on 18 August 2008, ten weeks before the white paper.[1] It hosts the paper,[2] and in 2009 Martti Malmi worked on its pages with Satoshi (their emails).

A copy of the site from 3 March 2009 is kept in nakamoto-archive.[3] The bitcoin.org repository on GitHub, which holds release announcements and network alerts from 2011 onward, is also part of this dataset.
