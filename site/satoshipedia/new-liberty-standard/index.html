From Satoshipedia, the Satoshi Mail encyclopedia

New Liberty Standard

Early exchanger and price-setter

Known for
An early bitcoin exchange rate

New Liberty Standard is the pseudonym of an early Bitcoin user who published an exchange rate for bitcoin. In February 2010 he explained on the forum: “I use a formula to calculate my exchange rate which is described on my exchange rate page” (the thread).

He is one of the most active participants in Satoshi's threads (his messages).
