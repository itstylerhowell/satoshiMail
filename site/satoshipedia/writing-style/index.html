Satoshi's writing has been studied closely for clues to his identity. Analyses point to British spellings, double-spaced sentences and a long daily gap in posting.[1] The figures below are computed live from the messages in this archive.

Spelling

His writing includes British forms such as “colour”, “grey” and “maths”, and the phrase “bloody hard”,[1] though he also uses American spellings.

Spacing

Satoshi usually put two spaces after a full stop, a typewriter-era habit:

Two spaces after a sentence: 1,014; one space: 277 (79% two).

None of this identifies anyone. Many people share each habit, and a careful writer could adopt them. See Identity of Satoshi Nakamoto.
