Satoshipedia

The Satoshi Mail encyclopedia: 52 articles about Satoshi Nakamoto, the people he wrote to, and the events of Bitcoin's first years. Every article links to the emails, posts and commits it draws on.

Archives

  • Blocktrainer archive Blocktrainer's searchable archive of Satoshi's emails and posts, with German translations.
  • The Quotable Satoshi SNI's collection of Satoshi quotes, which supplies the Topics in Satoshi Mail.
  • Satoshi Nakamoto Archive (satoshin.name) satoshin.name, a backup of Satoshi's BitcoinTalk posts and mailing-list emails, one page per message.
  • Satoshi Nakamoto Institute The organisation whose collection of Satoshi's writings is the core of this archive.
  • nakamoto-archive A GitHub archive of Satoshi's writings and primary sources, including private email.

Forums and lists

  • BitcoinTalk The forum Satoshi opened in November 2009, where he wrote most of his public posts.
  • Cryptography mailing list The mailing list where Satoshi announced the white paper and v0.1.
  • Forum staff board (2009) Deleted test topics from the Bitcoin forum's staff area, written before it opened in November 2009.
  • P2P Foundation The forum where Satoshi described Bitcoin for a general audience in February 2009.
  • SourceForge Where Bitcoin's code, releases and mailing list lived until 2011.
  • bitcoin-list Bitcoin's own announcement list on SourceForge, opened December 2008.
  • bitcoin.org The website Satoshi registered in 2008 to host the white paper and software.
