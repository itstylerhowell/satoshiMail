Wei Dai

From Satoshipedia, the Satoshi Mail encyclopedia

Wei Dai

Author of b-money and Crypto++

Known for
b-money (1998); the Crypto++ library
Contacted
August 2008

Wei Dai is a computer engineer who in 1998 published “b-money, an anonymous, distributed electronic cash system”.[1] B-money is reference [1] in the white paper.[2]

With Satoshi

After Adam Back pointed him to b-money, Satoshi wrote to Dai in August 2008: “I was very interested to read your b-money page. I'm getting ready to release a paper that expands on your ideas into a complete working system.” He asked for the year of publication so he could cite it. In January 2009 he wrote again to say he had released v0.1, which he thought “achieves nearly all the goals you set out to solve in your b-money paper” (the emails). Dai later published his copies of the emails, which give their dates: 22 August 2008 and 10 January 2009.[3]

In October 2023, answering questions from the solicitors for COPA in COPA v Wright, Dai said he had never sent Satoshi any code, that Satoshi had learned of b-money from Adam Back after drafting the paper, and that these emails were the whole of his correspondence with Satoshi.[4]

Crypto++

Dai also wrote Crypto++, a C++ cryptography library first released in 1995.[1] In 2010 Satoshi added part of it to Bitcoin for a faster SHA-256 (SVN r114).
