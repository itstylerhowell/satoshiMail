Wei Dai

Author of b-money and Crypto++

Known for
b-money (1998); the Crypto++ library
Contacted
August 2008

Wei Dai is a computer engineer who in 1998 published “b-money, an anonymous, distributed electronic cash system”.[1] B-money is reference [1] in the white paper.[2]

With Satoshi

After Adam Back pointed him to b-money, Satoshi wrote to Dai in August 2008: “I was very interested to read your b-money page. I'm getting ready to release a paper that expands on your ideas into a complete working system.” He asked for the year of publication so he could cite it. In January 2009 he wrote again to say he had released v0.1, which he thought “achieves nearly all the goals you set out to solve in your b-money paper” (the emails).

Crypto++

Dai also wrote Crypto++, a C++ cryptography library first released in 1995.[1] In 2010 Satoshi added part of it to Bitcoin for a faster SHA-256 (SVN r114).
