About Satoshi Mail

Every email, forum post and commit by Satoshi Nakamoto in a Gmail-style archive, with Satoshipedia and a map of his connections and timeline, 2008–2011.

Satoshi Mail is in beta and still in development. It was founded and made by Tyler Howell (@TylerHowell88), and it is open source under the MIT license: source code on GitHub.

What's here

  • 403 conversations: mailing lists, BitcoinTalk, the P2P Foundation and Satoshi's private email, with the replies around his messages.
  • 203 commits Satoshi made to Bitcoin's source code, each with its diff.
  • Satoshipedia: 52 articles on the people and events, linked to the messages they draw on.
  • Satoshi Map: everyone he corresponded with, and his timeline from 2008 to 2011.

Sources

Emails and forum posts from the Satoshi Nakamoto Institute; private correspondence from nakamoto-archive; commits from Bitcoin Core; the white paper from bitcoin.org. The archive was cross-checked against Blocktrainer's archive and satoshin.name, the Satoshi Nakamoto Archive: every message in either is also here. Satoshipedia's articles were written with AI assistance and cite their sources, including Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0).
