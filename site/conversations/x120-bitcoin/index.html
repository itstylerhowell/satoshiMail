Satoshi Nakamoto to Nicholas Bohm · 24 May 2009, 20:45 UTC · original
That's the transaction fee feature. If you generate a block containing a transaction that includes a transaction fee, you earn the transaction fee in addition to the 50.00. Someone must have included a 0.10 fee with one of their transactions. The setting to do that is under options. Including a fee is completely optional, more of a donation to nodes for helping out.
