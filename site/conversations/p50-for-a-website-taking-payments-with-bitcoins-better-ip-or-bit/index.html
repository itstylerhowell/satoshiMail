For a website taking payments with bitcoins, better: IP or bitcoin addresses?

BitcoinTalk forum · 6 messages · from 9 May 2010 · with Minsc, Satoshi Nakamoto, Xunie, dsg, dwdollar

Read in Satoshi Mail Archive source

Minsc · 9 May 2010, 04:44 UTC · original
For a website taking payments with bitcoins, which is better: taking payments through IP solely or using tons of bitcoin addresses that you have to reuse and reuse? And why?
dwdollar · 9 May 2010, 17:03 UTC · original
Right now, Bitcoin Addresses are the only option if you want automation.  If you're only taking donations, or only handling manual transactions, IP Addresses might be better suited.  That way they can send you comments or messages about their account info.
dsg · 9 May 2010, 17:09 UTC · original
Bitcoin addresses also provide better anonymity. In my opinion they are ideal. Otherwise it's necessary to use tor or the like also to guarantee anonymity.
Xunie · 14 May 2010, 01:16 UTC · original
Quote from: dsg on May 09, 2010, 05:09:38 PM
Bitcoin addresses also provide better anonymity. In my opinion they are ideal. Otherwise it's necessary to use tor or the like also to guarantee anonymity.
Isn't that "unsafe"? Say I am an exit node listening for bitcoin transactions and grab them? Or is everything public/private key encrypted?[1] [1]Which is my guess.
Xunie · 14 May 2010, 21:52 UTC · original
Quote from: soultcer on May 14, 2010, 07:58:57 PM
Actually no, transfering coins via IP address isn't encrypted. When you transfer coins to an IP, the recipient creates a new address just for that transaction and tells you to transfer coins to that address. A malicious exit node could sniff all Bitcoin traffic and intercept those transactions easily. So for everyone: DO NOT USE IP ADDRESSES AS DESTINATIONS, ALWAYS USE BITCOIN ADDRESSES.
I suggest we disable IP transactions while the user uses a Proxy! Just to be on the safe side.
