One Other Question

Private email with Dustin Trammell · 1 message · from 13 January 2009 · with Dustin D. Trammell

Read in Satoshi Mail Archive source

Dustin D. Trammell to satoshi@vistomail.com · 13 January 2009, 03:49 UTC · original
One other question I had... What prevents the single node with the most CPU power from generating and retaining the majority of the BitCoins? If every node is working independently of all others, if one is significantly more powerful than the others, isn't it probable that this node will reach the proper conclusion before other nodes? An underpowered node may get lucky once in a while, but if they are at a significant horsepower advantage I would expect the majority of BitCoins to be generated by the most powerful node. -- Dustin D.
