Update log

Every update to Satoshi Mail, newest first. Follow along with the RSS feed.

Open in Satoshi Mail

  1. 21 October 2026

    The envelope logo in Google and on phones

    • Satoshi Mail's envelope logo now comes in every size Google, browsers and phones look for, so search results can show it instead of a grey globe once Google recrawls the site.
    • Add Satoshi Mail to your phone's home screen and it gets the logo too.

  2. 21 October 2026

    Licenses and terms

    • The About page now lists the license and terms of everything in the archive: MIT for the code, CC BY-SA 4.0 for Satoshipedia, and each source's own terms for the messages.
    • Google's dataset listing for Satoshi Mail now links to those terms.

  3. 21 October 2026

    A bigger, easier Satoshi Map

    • Connections: zoom and pan the chart (scroll, pinch, drag or the + and − buttons); names stay readable and more appear as you zoom in.
    • Find anyone with the new “Find a person” box; picking someone zooms to them.
    • A bigger month slider with year marks and milestone dots you can jump to, and a compact key on the chart.
    • Timeline: one big chart with numbered milestone pins. Select any month to see what Satoshi wrote then, with links to every message and commit.
    • Arrow keys step through months, and Escape steps back out one level at a time.

  4. 21 October 2026

    Update log

    • This page: a log of every update to Satoshi Mail, newest first, with an RSS feed to follow along.

  5. 20 October 2026

    Easier to find, easier to read

    • Every page now has its own title and description in search results, and Google can match “SatoshiMail” as one word.
    • Clearer contrast for grey text, labels and badges, bigger touch targets for checkboxes, and tidier headings for screen readers.
    • The site is registered with Google Search Console and its sitemap submitted.

    Changes: c8fff6d, d82d386, 4ea5e89, d7de37d

  6. 20 October 2026

    Faster loading

    • The first visit downloads less than half as much: commit diffs load only when you open a commit or search, and only the icons in use are downloaded.
    • Repeat visits load almost instantly, because the app's files are now cached for a year and renamed whenever they change.

    Changes: 01f42ee

  7. 20 October 2026

    Satoshi by name

    • The message list says “Satoshi” where it used to say “me”, as in “Satoshi, Hal 3”. Searching from:me still works.

    Changes: 71867ae

  8. 20 October 2026

    New home at satoshimail.vercel.app

    • Satoshi Mail now lives at satoshimail.vercel.app, and everything needed to rebuild it is on GitHub.
    • Visits are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't record what you search for.
    • The “page not found” page keeps its styling at any address.

    Changes: 5e2ba6a, 42b9158, 9beadaf, 5649fc9

  9. 20 October 2026

    Exact dates from the correspondents' own copies

    • Satoshi's five emails with Adam Back now have their exact send times, from Back's copies filed in COPA v Wright.
    • Satoshi's second email to Wei Dai is correctly dated 10 January 2009, from Dai's own copies.
    • Satoshipedia's Adam Back and Wei Dai articles cite both, including Dai's 2023 answers to COPA's lawyers.
    • A credit to Luke Igel and Riley Walz's Jmail, which inspired Satoshi Mail, in the sidebar, About and the beta notice.

    Changes: 62edda7, f76e28b, 0d94a6f

  10. 20 October 2026

    A 2010–2013 Gmail look

    • Arial, square corners, grey buttons, the red COMPOSE button and yellow highlights, as Gmail looked when Satoshi was writing.

    Changes: a878e56

  11. 20 October 2026

    New material from public records

    • Satoshi's 2009 emails with Nicholas Bohm: 16 conversations and 64 emails, 25 of them by Satoshi, from Bohm's evidence in COPA v Wright.
    • Three test topics from the Bitcoin forum's staff area, written before it opened in November 2009: 30 posts, 20 by Satoshi.
    • The exact time of Satoshi's last email to Gavin Andresen, with Gavin's reply.
    • New Satoshipedia articles: Nicholas Bohm, COPA v Wright, Kleiman v. Wright and the forum staff board.

    Changes: c4d508e

  12. 20 October 2026

    Send feedback, and a simpler app

    • Send feedback from Settings or Satoshipedia: it drafts a post to @TylerHowell88 on X.
    • A Sources page under Settings → Archive lists where everything comes from, instead of source chips on every conversation.
    • The density setting, the apps grid and the People and More apps were removed.

    Changes: 33d9f0c, cb84e6b, b4d3af9, b6a6a41, 069a119, aa6f327

  13. 20 October 2026

    AI Overview in search

    • Searches like “Hal Finney” or “did Satoshi have any photos” get a short AI-written overview, answered only from this archive and Satoshipedia, with links to the sources.

    Changes: 0c069e0

  14. 20 October 2026

    A new look, inspired by Jmail

    • Jmail's colours, rows and app dock, with an overview banner at the top of search results.

    Changes: 0d506b8, 9f36ea2

  15. 20 October 2026

    Cross-checked against satoshin.name

    • Every one of satoshin.name's 567 posts and emails was matched to the archive, and Satoshi Mail has six BitcoinTalk posts it lacks.

    Changes: 57d7744

  16. 20 October 2026

    Open source launch

    • Satoshi Mail, Satoshipedia and Satoshi Map, open source under the MIT license: every email, forum post and commit by Satoshi Nakamoto, 2008–2011.

    Changes: ea26fbd
